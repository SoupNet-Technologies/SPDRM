# Code of Conduct

Be decent. Concretely:

- Critique code and ideas, not people. Ruthless is fine for merge conflicts and
  regex minifiers; apply it to neither contributors nor yourself.
- No harassment, no bigotry, no spam. Standard, obvious, and still worth saying.
- Assume people are doing this for fun. They are. So are you, hopefully.

Enforcement: maintainers may remove comments, close issues, and block accounts
that make the project worse to be around. Report problems through GitHub's
private contact or a private email to the address in the repo owner's profile.

This policy is deliberately short because everything it needs to say fits in
four lines. Most Codes of Conduct are longer; most violations are the same.
